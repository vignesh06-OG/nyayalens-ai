RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Residential Rental Agreement is entered into on 1 April 2026 between
Ramesh Kumar ("Landlord") and Sunita Sharma ("Tenant") for Flat 4B, Sunrise
Apartments, Nashik, Maharashtra.

1. RENT. The Tenant shall pay Rs 25,000 per month in advance on or before the
5th day of each month. Late payment attracts interest at 2% per month.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Tenant has paid Rs 5,00,000 as a security deposit,
which may be forfeited at the sole discretion of the Landlord without any
reason whatsoever. The deposit shall be refunded within 60 days of vacating.

3. MAINTENANCE. The Tenant shall maintain the premises in good condition and
shall indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord from unlimited liability for
any damage, without limitation.

4. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written
notice. The Landlord may terminate at its sole discretion without notice.

5. RENEWAL. This agreement renews automatically for 11 months unless either
party objects in writing 60 days before expiry.

6. FORCE MAJEURE. The Landlord shall not be liable for acts of God, floods, or
epidemics rendering the premises unusable.

Signed,
Ramesh Kumar (Landlord)      Sunita Sharma (Tenant)
